See the [2026-10-18 image-level correction](docs/RIGHTS_CORRECTION_20261018.md).
An article's CC BY licence does not automatically cover third-party figures
with separate permission credits. The corrected Supplementary S1 takes precedence
over blanket source-image licence descriptions in earlier snapshots.

  License; see `LICENSE`.
- Repository-authored documentation, derived figures, and tabular metadata:
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), unless a file
  states otherwise.
- Bibliographic facts, identifiers, and source links are supplied for
  provenance. Their inclusion does not grant rights to the referenced works.
- Third-party electron micrographs and publisher figures are not distributed
  in this repository and are not relicensed. Consult `data/sources.csv` and
  `supplementary/supplementary_S1_data_licenses.csv` before obtaining or using
  source material.
