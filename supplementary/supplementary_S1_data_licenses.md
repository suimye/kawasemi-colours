# Supplementary S1 — Source licences and local-analysis record

This table lists the real electron-micrograph panels used in local model
development, together with their recorded source and licence status. It is a
provenance record, not legal advice. Users must check the source record and the
law applicable to their own use.

## Public-release decision

- The public repository distributes **no source electron-micrograph pixels**,
  regardless of the licence recorded for the source paper.
- CC BY, CC BY-NC, green-open-access, and all-rights-reserved records remain in
  the metadata only so that the local analysis population is auditable.
- No licence recorded in this table is extended or replaced by the repository's
  MIT or CC BY 4.0 licences.
