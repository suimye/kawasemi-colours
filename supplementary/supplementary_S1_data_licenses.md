# Supplementary S1 — Image-level source licences

This table records the 31 real-image records used in local analysis. No original
electron-micrograph pixels are distributed in this public repository. Inclusion
of provenance metadata does not grant permission to obtain or redistribute images.
Analysis eligibility is documented separately in Supplementary S3–S4.

The 2026-10-18 correction distinguishes article-level licences from third-party
figure credits. Nine review-derived records have permission credits without
documented image-level CC BY permission; two other review-derived records retain
their original-source CC BY credit without inferring version 4.0 from the review.
Non-commercial status alone is not a blanket authorization for training use.
See [correction details](../docs/RIGHTS_CORRECTION_20261018.md).

The CSV includes original source DOIs and notes. All source images remain excluded
from this repository regardless of their recorded licence. The software MIT licence
and the CC BY 4.0 licence for author-created material do not relicense source images.
